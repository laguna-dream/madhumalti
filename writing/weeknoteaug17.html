when i want to not look at my laptop and i feel the anxiety start to creep i try to step out of the house. or go on the terrace and walk around. it's a wide and green view with a hill on one side.

i am realising that all the normie advice of... eat well, keep moving, talk to people, and get out of your own head.. does a lot of heavy lifting. it's much easier to function and figure when i'm doing this and not self sabotaging and ruminating. it's a process. i forget about it a lot.

sometimes it's hard. there have been not so good days and a few really bad days. wherever you go, there you are and all that.

but today was a good day and i'm glad i was here. things did not feel so heavy. i listened to cigarette burns by flunk and walked around and got a little sleepy. then i listened to against all logic - i never dream and felt less sleepy.

i made a little list of things that constitute 'movement' for me: driving, going to the beach, going for a walk, going to the terrace, going for a run, yoga, dancing, doing chores and cleaning, cooking, squats, qi gong, dry brushing, self massage. whenever i feel the ((dread)) start to creep in i try to do one of these.

worked a bit more.

around 6 i usually step out for a walk/drive to the beach. i can go on the beach or when i dont feel like getting sandy i can go to the chapel compound overlooking the beach and sit and watch kids play football, tourists take photos, couples frolicking in the water, regular local uncles conversing in a corner, teenagers playing cards in another corner, someone going for a run, someone attempting to wade knee deep across the beach creek (there is no graceful way to do this), dogs running around and swimming and shaking themselves dry, someone drinking from a paper bag next to me, someone meditating, etc etc. i hang around here for a bit until the sun starts to dip (no clear horizon sunsets because of the clouds and sporadic rain) and head back. sometimes i stop at nidhi or calisto to pick up something fun.

it's off season and there's not so much 'to do' yet, and not a whole lot of people around. though the monsoons have been a lot lighter than i expected. it rains for a bit throughout the day but never too heavy and never very long.

sometimes living in a little house by the sea away from people can get a bit lonely when you're feeling low. i'm staying with a friend, though she was gone for 2 weeks recently and just got back home tonight. the other friends i have in goa stay at least 40-50 minutes of a drive away, which is usually a once a week trip for something or the other that's going on. sometimes people visit and that is always nice. i miss having my own place to host people over.

i'm reminded of the fact that i like being around people. i have had phases in life where i've distanced myself from the people around me (hello immediate post-covid era) out of growing pains, a desire to not be known, disappear, become someone else, not be hurt, that somehow i'd be able to just figure out my life alone and that i didn't need anyone. this was quite unnecessary and painful. i sometimes feel the itch to do that when things are er, not going well, but i have to remind myself that this does not end well.

so i call a friend or find a place to drink tea and people watch or play with frida and tell her i love her or work on some unfinished draft of something instead. or i lie in bed till the feeling passes. watch something. sometimes i smoke a cigarette.
